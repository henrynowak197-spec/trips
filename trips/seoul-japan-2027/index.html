Fri, Jan 15

Depart New York (JFK)

Flight New York JFK → Seoul ICN
⏱ ≈ 15h 40m nonstop$1,300–2,000 whole multi-city ticket (JFK→ICN, HND→JFK) est source

Korean Air flies JFK–ICN nonstop. Its fare page shows January 2027 round trips from about $1,499. Multi-city (JFK→ICN, HND→JFK) prices vary, so this is an estimate. You cross the date line and land on Jan 16. secondary source source

  • Fill in Korea's e-Arrival Card within the 3 days before you land (see Before you go).

Sat, Jan 16 · Seoul

Land at Incheon, into Seoul

Train Incheon Airport → Seoul Station
⏱ ≈ 43–51 min (AREX Express)$8.62–9.67 ₩11,600–13,000 secondary source source

₩13,000 standard adult fare, ₩11,600 with discounted tickets. One source still lists ₩9,500, so check airportrailroad.com. secondary source source

  • Buy and top up T-money cards at the airport convenience store.
  • Easy evening near the hotel.
Local tips
  • Get a T-money card for subway and buses. The base subway fare is ₩1,550 by card (cash single tickets cost more), and card users get transfer discounts. secondary source source
  • The Climate Card's status for tourists changed in 2026 and sources conflict, so check before buying one; T-money pay-as-you-go always works. secondary source source
  • Cards are widely accepted, but keep some won in cash for street food and market stalls. unverified

Wed, Jan 20 · Tokyo

Fly Seoul Gimpo → Tokyo Haneda

Flight Seoul Gimpo (GMP) → Tokyo Haneda (HND)
⏱ ≈ 2h 10m$160–300 one way est source

Gimpo is close to central Seoul and lands at Haneda, near central Tokyo. Fares online start around $158, but January fares weren't published, so this is an estimate. secondary source source

Train Haneda Airport → Shinagawa
⏱ ≈ 11–20 min (Keikyu)$1.91–2.10 ¥300–330 secondary source source

Have your Visit Japan Web QR code ready for immigration and customs. secondary source source

  • Check in, then an evening in Shibuya or Shinjuku.
Local tips
  • From Haneda, the Keikyu line reaches Shinagawa in about 11–20 minutes for roughly ¥300–330; sources differ slightly on the exact fare. secondary source source
  • On an iPhone you can add a Suica to Apple Wallet and top it up with a card, so you can tap through gates without buying a physical card. unverified
  • Tax-free shopping is now a refund: you pay tax in the shop and claim it at customs when you leave Japan, before check-in. Keep the goods with you and allow extra airport time. source

Sat, Jan 23 · Hakuba

Tokyo → Nagano → Hakuba

Shinkansen Tokyo → Nagano
⏱ ≈ 80–105 min$53 ¥8,340 reserved seat secondary source source

Kagayaki is all-reserved and fastest; Hakutaka also has unreserved cars (about ¥7,680–7,810). secondary source source

Bus Nagano Station (East Exit, stop 26) → Hakuba
⏱ ≈ 60–75 min$14.01–15.29 ¥2,200–2,400 2024–25 fare secondary source source

Some winter buses are reservation-only, with one bus and no extra capacity. Book online up to 2 months ahead. The 2026–27 timetable wasn't out when this was checked. secondary source source

  • Pick up rental gear in the afternoon (many shops let you collect the day before).
  • Onsen and an early night.
Local tips
  • Japan Post's Ski Yu-Pack can deliver boards and bags to your hotel by the day before you ride. source
  • Luggage forwarding (takkyubin) usually takes 1–2 days, so send big bags from Tokyo before you leave. secondary source source
  • Lift prices are dynamic and can change depending on how early you buy, so buy online ahead. secondary source source
