Local tips
  • Bring a clear bag or nothing; bags are X-rayed or hand-searched at the gates. source
  • Clear bag policy: one clear bag up to 12 × 6 × 12 in, or a small clutch up to 4.5 × 6.5 in. Leave your suitcase in the car. source
  • On-site parking is prepaid only; if your ride doesn't have a pass, use Park & Ride instead. secondary source source
  • Rideshare drop-off and pickup are in a designated lot, not at the gates, and can be a long walk. Surge pricing after the game is likely. secondary source source
2
Mon, Jan 4 · Pompano Beach

Beach day in Pompano

  • Recover from the game. Beach, then walk out on Fisher Family Pier.
  • Lunch or dinner at the Pompano Fishing Village.
  • Optional: Hillsboro Inlet Park for a view of the lighthouse.
Local tips
  • Fisher Family Pier runs about 900 ft out from the beach; the Pompano Fishing Village next to it has restaurants on the sand. secondary source source
  • January is dry, with highs in the low-to-mid 70s °F and lows around 56–64 °F. The ocean is usually about 74–77 °F. secondary source source
  • Hillsboro Inlet Lighthouse can only be toured by boat on a few set days a year, so check the preservation society's schedule; otherwise view it from Hillsboro Inlet Park. secondary source source

Drive up to Port St. Lucie

Car Pompano Beach → Port St. Lucie
⏱ ≈ 1 h 40 min, about 80 miprice not found — add it in the budget unverified

I-95 has no tolls on this stretch; the Turnpike is usually smoother but tolled. I couldn't confirm the toll amount. secondary source source

  • Easy afternoon and evening with family and friends.
Local tips
  • Port St. Lucie is inland; the beaches are on Hutchinson Island to the east. secondary source source
  • The drive from Pompano is about 80 miles and 1 h 40 min by I-95 or Florida's Turnpike. The Turnpike has tolls; cars without SunPass are billed by plate with extra fees. secondary source source

Wed, Jan 6 · Port St. Lucie

  • Afternoon on Hutchinson Island: Fort Pierce Inlet State Park, Jetty Park or Pepper Park.
