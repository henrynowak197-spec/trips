Ski day: Sugar Mountain (Tue)

Car (friends) Charlotte → Sugar Mountain
⏱ ≈ 2 h 15–20 min, 105–113 mi

Leave by about 7 AM for a full day. Mountain roads can ice up; check NCDOT if it snowed. secondary source source
