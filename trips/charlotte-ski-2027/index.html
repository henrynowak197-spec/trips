  • Mint Museum: Free Wednesday Evenings (Uptown and Randolph). Uptown is open until 9 PM on Wednesdays. source

Thu, Jan 21 · Sugar Mountain

Ski day 1: Sugar Mountain (Thu)

Car (friends) Charlotte → Sugar Mountain
⏱ ≈ 2 h 15–20 min, 105–113 mi

Leave by about 7 AM to get a full day. NC 105 / US 321 through Boone; mountain roads can ice up, so check NCDOT if it snowed. secondary source source

  • Weekday full-day ticket $65 plus a $60 rental set. That's the cheapest weekday full day at the biggest NC resort. source
  • Cheaper option: App Ski Mountain's 8 Hour Flex is $57 weekday plus a $47 rental (helmet included). source
  • Look at the resort's snow report the night before. Southern resorts rely on snowmaking, so open terrain changes a lot.
Local tips
  • 2026-27 adult tickets: full day $65 weekday / $104 weekend; night $47 / $60. MLK Monday is charged at the midweek rate, but Jan 16–17 are weekend rates. source
  • Snowboard rental (full set): $60 full day weekday, $69 weekends. Helmet $26. source
  • The college rate ($53 full day) is for NC and TN students only, so a New York student ID won't qualify. source
  • About 16 miles (≈ 25 min) from Boone. secondary source source

Sat, Jan 23 · Crowders Mountain

Crowders Mountain hike (Sat)

Car (friends) Charlotte → Crowders Mountain
⏱ ≈ 40 min, about 33 mi west

Saturday lots fill up, so get there early. secondary source source

  • Free state park. Hike Kings Pinnacle or the Rocktop / Crowders summit for views back to the Charlotte skyline.
  • Evening: Restaurant Week dinner, or a cheap night in.
Local tips
  • Free to enter. The Ridgeline Trail starts at the Visitor Center with a short spur to Kings Pinnacle (1,705 ft). secondary source source
  • Rocktop Trail: 5.9 miles one way, 2–4 hours, moderate to hard. Lots fill up on weekends, so go early on Saturday. secondary source source

Sun, Jan 24 · App Ski Mountain

Ski day 2: App Ski night session (Sun)

Car (friends) Charlotte → Blowing Rock
⏱ ≈ 2 h

Leave around 2:30 PM, ride 5–10 PM, back in Charlotte around midnight. Have someone well rested drive home. secondary source source

  • Sunday night counts as Sun–Thu: $40 adult or $33 student (class schedule and ID). The rental is $37–41 with a helmet included. source
  • Afternoon option: a walk around Blowing Rock or Boone before the lifts open for night skiing.
Local tips
  • 2026-27 adult, bought online: Night 5–10 PM is $40 Sun–Thu and $50 Fri/Sat. The 8 Hour Flex is $57 weekday, and the Super Session (9 AM till close) is $66 weekday. Walk-up adds $2. source
  • Student column: Night Sun–Thu $33, 8 Hour weekday $46. You need a class schedule and a photo ID at the window. Not valid on holidays. source
  • Snowboard rental includes a helmet: night $37 weekday / $41 weekend, 8 Hour $47 / $51. Reserve online ahead. source
  • Jan 16 and 17 are holiday rates for day tickets. Jan 18 (MLK Monday) is a regular weekday. source

Notes

  • Recommended dates: Tue Jan 19 – Tue Jan 26. Ski days land on weekday or Sun-night rates instead of MLK weekend rates (Sugar full day is $65 weekday vs $104 weekend). You catch two Hornets home games, one of them Star Wars Night, and Restaurant Week (from Jan 22). Midweek flights are often cheaper.
  • If you go Jan 16–23 instead: you still get the Jan 22 Hornets game and the start of Restaurant Week. Ski on Mon Jan 18 (MLK Day is a regular weekday rate at Sugar, App Ski and Beech) and avoid Jan 16–17 holiday/weekend prices.
  • Staying with friends who have a car, so lodging is $0. The budget is flights, your gas share, lift tickets, rentals, games and food.
  • Flights, gas, food, Hornets tickets and the thank-you are estimates (est). Everything else links to the page I checked on 2026-11-23.
