Sun, Mar 28 · Muir Woods

Redwoods day: Muir Woods with your cousin

Car Berkeley → Muir Woods
⏱ ≈ 1 h each way$8.50–9 Richmond–San Rafael Bridge toll, westbound only est source

Go out via I-580 and the Richmond–San Rafael Bridge, then US-101 and Hwy 1. Coming back the same way is toll-free. Coming back through SF instead costs a Golden Gate Bridge toll ($10.25 FasTrak). secondary source source

  • Mar 28 is Easter Sunday, so book the earliest parking slot you can; the lot fills on busy days.
  • Main Trail loop to Cathedral Grove (about 2 miles, 1.5–2 h). Add the Hillside or Ben Johnson trail if you want a real hike.
  • Optional on the way back: Sausalito or the Marin Headlands view of the Golden Gate (Golden Gate toll if you return through SF).
Local tips
  • Reservation fees still apply on park fee-free days; full refund if you cancel at least 72 hours ahead. source
  • A parking or shuttle reservation is required, plus a $15 entrance fee for ages 16+. source
  • The Main Trail loop is about 2 miles and 1.5–2 hours; Cathedral Grove is a designated quiet zone. source
  • Open 8:00 a.m. until about sunset; cell service is poor, so load your reservation before you go. source
